# Short-form video: hooks, scripts, strategy

## Hook library
**Curiosity (engagement)**: "The secret to [outcome] nobody talks about" · "I found the hidden feature in [product] that changes everything" · "I tried [thing] for 30 days and was NOT expecting this" · "Why does nobody talk about this?" · "Am I the only one who didn't know this?"
**Value (saves)**: "How to [outcome] in [timeframe]" · "[N] [things] that will [benefit]" · "Everything about [topic] in 60 seconds" · "[Outcome] hack that actually works" · "If you're struggling with [problem], watch this" · "Stop doing [practice] — here's why" · "[N] mistakes killing your [results]".
**Story (watch time)**: "3 months ago I [bad state]. Today I [good state]." · "How I went from [before] to [after]" · "I used to think [belief]. Then [event] changed everything." · "I made a huge mistake with [topic]" · "So this just happened…"
**Controversial (comments)**: "Unpopular opinion: [statement]" · "[Common advice] is actually wrong" · "Most [audience] get this completely wrong".

## Scripting template
```markdown
## Video: [Working title]
Platform: TikTok / Reels / Shorts · Length: XX s · Format: talking head / slideshow / demo / screen recording

### Hook (0–3 s)
Visual: … | Audio: … | Text overlay: …
### Body (3–X s)
[ts] – what happens / what you say
### CTA (final 3–5 s)
Verbal: … | Text: … | Action: follow / comment / link in bio
### Production notes
Music or trending sound · B-roll needed · Graphics and text animations
```

## Structures
- **Problem-solution (15–30 s)**: 0–3 s problem · 3–10 s why it matters · 10–25 s solution · 25–30 s CTA.
- **List (30–60 s)**: hook · one item every 5–8 s · CTA.
- **Tutorial (30–60 s)**: result first · "here's how" · steps · result + CTA.
- **Story arc (45–60 s)**: 0–3 s tease outcome · 3–15 s setup and stakes · 15–45 s what happened · 45–55 s resolution · 55–60 s lesson/CTA. Best for personal stories, cases, testimonials.
- **POV/skit (15–30 s)**: 0–3 s text overlay sets the scene · 3–25 s act out the scenario · 25–30 s punchline. Best for relatable humor and niche communities.

## Visual patterns
**Talking head**: good light (ring or window), eye contact, hand gestures, interesting background.
**Slideshow**: strong visual per slide (2–4 s), text overlays, consistent style, voiceover or trending sound.
**Screen recording**: zoom on the important area, cursor highlight or click animation, smooth intentional movement, optional face in the corner.
**B-roll heavy**: show don't tell, 1–3 s cuts, cuts matched to voiceover beats, mix wide/medium/close.

## Audio
Trending sounds: for entertainment/lifestyle when the sound fits and the trend is still rising; skip when it distracts or is declining. Original audio: educational content, storytimes, demos, tutorials, building a recognizable voice.
Voiceover: slightly faster than conversation, varied tone, pauses on key points, quiet room + noise removal; synthetic voices work for faceless content.
Music: match energy to content, avoid copyrighted tracks on Reels/Shorts, prefer platform libraries, duck music under voiceover.

## Posting
Times (test): TikTok 7–9am, 12–3pm, 7–11pm · Reels 9am, 12pm, 7–9pm · Shorts 12–3pm, 7–9pm (in {{profile.timezone}}).
Frequency: growing — 1/day minimum, 2–4/day optimal; maintaining — 3/week minimum, 1/day optimal; testing — 2–5/week.
Batch workflow: ideate 10–20 concepts (30 min) → script 5–10 videos (1 h) → film everything (2 h) → edit + captions (2–3 h) → schedule (30 min).

## Analytics and iteration
Watch-time % (engaging throughout?) · completion rate (hook + content delivered?) · saves (worth revisiting?) · shares (worth spreading?) · comments (sparked conversation?) · follows (wanted more?).
Test: hooks (same content, different opening), length (15/30/60 s), format, posting time, CTA.
Pivot when: 5+ videos under ~1% completion → change hooks; high views but low follows → fix CTA and audience fit; high saves but low shares → valuable but not social; many negative comments → adjust tone.
