# Post templates and hook library

## LinkedIn

**Story post**
```
[Hook: unexpected outcome or lesson]
[Set the scene: when/where]
[The challenge]
[What you tried / what happened]
[Turning point]
[Result]
[Lesson for the reader]
[Question to prompt engagement]
```

**Contrarian take**
```
[Unpopular opinion, stated boldly]
Here's why:
[Reason 1] [Reason 2] [Reason 3]
[What to do instead]
[Invite discussion: "Am I wrong?"]
```

**List post**
```
[X things I learned about [topic] after [credibility builder]:]
1. [Point] — [brief explanation]
2. …
[Wrap-up insight]
Which resonates most?
```

**How-to**
```
How to [outcome] in [timeframe]:
Step 1: [Action]
  ↳ [Why it matters]
Step 2: [Action]
  ↳ [Key detail]
Step 3: [Action]
  ↳ [Common mistake to avoid]
[Expected result]
[CTA or question]
```

## X / Twitter threads

**Tutorial**: T1 hook + promise ("Here's exactly how to [outcome], step by step:") → T2–T7 one step each → final summary + follow CTA.
**Story**: T1 intriguing hook ("[Time] ago, [unexpected thing] happened. Here's the full story:") → T2–T6 story beats building tension → T7 resolution + lesson → final takeaway + engagement ask.
**Breakdown**: T1 "[Company] just [did thing]. Here's why it's smart (and what you can learn):" → T2–T6 analysis points → T7 key takeaway + follow CTA.

## Instagram

**Carousel**: slide 1 bold statement or question → slides 2–9 one point each (visual + text) → slide 10 summary + CTA. Caption expands the topic and repeats the CTA.
**Reel script**: hook 0–2 s (pattern interrupt or bold claim) → setup 2–5 s → value 5–25 s → CTA 25–30 s.

## Hook library
**Curiosity**: "I was wrong about [belief]." · "The real reason [outcome] happens isn't what you think." · "[Result] — and it only took [time]." · "Nobody talks about [insider knowledge]."
**Story**: "Last week, [unexpected thing] happened." · "I almost [failure]." · "3 years ago I [past]. Today [present]." · "[Person] told me something I'll never forget."
**Value**: "How to [outcome] (without [pain]):" · "[N] [things] that [outcome]:" · "The simplest way to [outcome]:" · "Stop [mistake]. Do this instead:"
**Contrarian**: "Unpopular opinion: [statement]" · "[Common advice] is wrong. Here's why:" · "I stopped [practice] and [result]." · "Everyone says [X]. The truth is [Y]."
**Social proof**: "We [result] in [timeframe]. Here's the story:" · "[N] people asked me about [topic]. Here's my answer:" · "[Authority] taught me [lesson]."
Use only claims and numbers the client can back up.
