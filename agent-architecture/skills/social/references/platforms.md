# Platform strategy and limits

## LinkedIn
B2B, thought leadership, networking, recruiting. Professionals and decision-makers. 3–5x/week; Tue–Thu 7–8am, 12pm, 5–6pm.
Works: personal stories with business lessons; contrarian industry takes; behind the scenes of building; original data; carousels (document posts); discussion polls.
Doesn't: overly promotional content; generic motivational quotes; links in the post body (kills reach); corporate speak.
Format: first line is everything (hook before "see more"); line breaks; 1,200–1,500 chars performs well; links in comments; tag sparingly and genuinely.
Algorithm: first-hour engagement matters most; comments > reactions > clicks; dwell time signals quality; no external links in body; document posts get strong reach; polls drive engagement but not authority.

## X / Twitter
Tech, media, real-time commentary, community. 3–10x/day including replies; best times vary — test.
Works: hot takes; threads that teach; behind the scenes; engaging with others; on-brand humor; live commentary.
Doesn't: pure self-promotion; threads without a hook; ignoring replies; scheduling everything.
Format: tweets under 100 chars get more engagement; thread = hook, promise, deliver; quote tweets with added insight beat plain retweets; visuals stop the scroll.
Algorithm: replies and quote tweets build authority; threads keep people on platform; images/video get more reach; first 30 minutes matter; premium may boost reach.

## Instagram
Visual brands, lifestyle, e-commerce, 18–44. 1–2 feed posts/day, 3–10 Stories; 11am–1pm, 7–9pm.
Works: strong visuals; behind-the-scenes Stories; Reels; value carousels; user-generated content; interactive Stories (polls, questions).
Doesn't: low-quality images; text-stuffed images; ignoring Stories/Reels; only promotion.
Format: Reels get ~2x reach of static; first frame must hook; carousels up to 10 educational slides; use all Story features.
Algorithm: Reels prioritized; saves and shares > likes; Stories keep you top of feed; consistency over perfection.

## TikTok
Awareness, 16–34, entertainment. 1–4x/day; 7–9am, 12–3pm, 7–11pm.
Works: native unpolished content; trending sounds and formats; education in an entertaining wrapper; POV and day-in-the-life; video replies to comments; duets and stitches.
Doesn't: over-produced content; ignoring trends; hard selling; repurposed horizontal video.
Format: hook in 1–2 s; start under 30 s; vertical only; trending sounds; consistent posting trains the algorithm.

## Facebook
Communities, local business, 25–55+. 1–2x/day; 1–4pm weekdays.
Works: Groups; native video; live video; local content and events; discussion questions.
Doesn't: external links (reach killer); pure promotion; ignoring comments; unadapted cross-posting.

## Limits and counts
| Platform | Max hashtags | Recommended | Max chars | Visible before "more" |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | 5 | 3–5 | 2,200 | ~125 |
| Facebook | no limit | 1–2 | 63,206 | 40–80 ideal for engagement |
| TikTok | 5 | 3–5 | 4,000 | ~150 |
| LinkedIn | no limit | 3–5 | 3,000 | ~210 |
| X | no limit | 1–2 | 280 (25,000 premium) | full tweet |
| YouTube | 15 (over = all ignored) | 3–5 | title 100 (~70 visible), description 5,000 | ~100 |
| Pinterest | 20 | 2–5 | title 100, description 500 | ~50 desktop |
| Threads | 1 topic tag | 1 | 500 (10,500 with text attachment) | 1–2 lines |

Notes: hashtags count toward character limits; front-load the message before truncation; on Instagram and TikTok fewer hashtags outperform stuffing; on LinkedIn hashtags at the end beat inline; on Facebook hashtags barely affect reach; YouTube shows the first 3 description hashtags above the title (1–5 for Shorts); Pinterest has deprioritized hashtags — write keywords as natural sentences.
